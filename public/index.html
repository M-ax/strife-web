PREEMPTIVE TECH SUPPORT

Fair
questions.

Yes, the name is on purpose.

Is this actually a Discord replacement?+

For jumping into voice and watching something with your friends? That's the idea. Strife brings Mumble voice, channel chat, and Helltube into one desktop window. It isn't trying to recreate every Discord feature. We have other things to procrastinate on.

Do I need to run my own servers?+

You need access to a Mumble/Murmur server and a Helltube server. Run your own or join servers someone else operates. Strife is the client; it doesn't include a hosted community service or set up servers for you. The getting-started wiki walks through hosting, and the server scripts automate the setup.

Will it work with my existing Mumble server?+

Yes. Strife uses the native Mumble client for capture, audio processing, networking, and global shortcuts. Vanilla servers don't need a special plugin or a Strife-shaped hole punched in them.

Do images show up in channel chat?+

Yes. The current downloads show incoming embedded images inline, sized to the chat pane. Linked images keep their targets. Unsupported or unreadable images show a text placeholder; remote image URLs are not loaded as attachments.

What's new in the current build?+

The current downloads add a chat image viewer, a dockable Downloads panel, and Helltube room and video-mute controls. With an updated Helltube server, HLS playback goes directly to its HTTPS media backend; sign-in and controls still use Strife's private local proxy. See the desktop media and download setup notes.

Browser screen sharing remains available. The experimental native OBS sharing helper is not packaged in the installers or archives.

Is my microphone going to sound like a leaf blower?+

RNNoise microphone noise suppression is included and enabled by default. Input devices, processing, and push-to-talk bindings are configured in the native voice settings. Results still depend on your microphone. We're software, not an exorcist.

Where does my data go?+

Your voice and chat go through the Mumble server you connect to; video and its account live on your chosen Helltube server. Their operators control those services. Strife keeps its settings locally. This landing page uses no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or cookies; hosting providers may still retain request logs.

Can I look under the hood?+

Please do. The source is on GitHub. The app is built with PhotinoX and the native Mumble client, with Helltube providing the video interface. Bugs belong in the issue tracker. Hot takes can stay in voice.
